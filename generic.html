About Me

I am a software engineer in my early twenties with a passion for technology, economics, finance, psychology, philosophy, linguistics, business, nature, music, sports, and true crime. I spend most of my time working, volunteering, studying, and spending time with my cat, Jack.

I enjoy traveling, hiking, climbing, yoga, reading, photography, playing saxophone, video games, board games, musicals, watching movies, and listening to music and podcasts.

University of Kansas

I began attending the University of Kansas during the Fall 2019 semester, where I majored in Computer Science. Shortly after beginning my journey at KU, I switched my major to Interdisciplinary Computing, combining a Computer Science degree with an Economics degree. Along with this, I chose to minor in Mathematics.

I graduated in the Spring of 2023 from the University of Kansas's School of Engineering with my Bachelor of Science in Interdisciplinary Computer Science and Economics and my minor in Mathematics. While an undergraduate at KU, I was a member of the Association of Computing Machinery (ACM) Club, Solar Car KU, Chess Club, Economics Club, and HEAL KU - student led organization promoting mental health and self care. I began a full-time job as a Software Engineer following the completion of my degree.

Rocky Mountain Wildlife Alliance

Following my relocation to Colorado, I wanted to continue working with animals, primarily wildlife, and getting involved in my community. I began volunteering at Rocky Mountain Wildlife Alliance (RMWA) in April of 2024 under Emily Davenport, a Certified Wildlife Rehabilitator and Registered Veterinary Technician.

At RMWA I intake patients and perform initial screenings, provide diet and food prep requirements, formulate and administer various medications (orally and subcutaneously), enrich habitats per species requirements, pariticipate in animal captures/releases, attend programs and educate communities, assist in medical procedures such as wrapping wings, gavaging, and euthanasia, and engage in ongoing training and professional development.

Denver Zoo Conservation Alliance

Along with beginning my journey at Rocky Mountain Wildlife Alliance, I began volunteering at the Denver Zoo Conservation Alliance (formerly Denver Zoo) in April of 2024, as well.
