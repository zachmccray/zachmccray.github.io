## About Me ##
I am a software engineer in my early twenties with a passion for technology, economics, finance, psychology, philosophy, neuroscience, linguistics, business, nature, music, sports, and true crime. I spend most of my time working, volunteering, studying, and spending time with my cat, Jack.

I graduated from the University of Kansas’s School of Engineering in May 2023 studying Computer Science, Economics, and Mathematics. While an undergraduate at KU, I was a member of the Association of Computing Machinery (ACM) Club, Solar Car KU, Chess Club, Economics Club, & HEAL KU - student led organization promoting mental health and self care.

I am currently taking courses to continue studying foreign languages, namely French (A2) and Japanese (A2), while I continue to self-study Spanish (B2) Chinese (A2), and American Sign Language (Level 2). I plan to pursue my Master's in Computer Science, Statistics, Finance, or Business.

Outside of work and school, I spend time volunteering with a handful of different organizations. I enjoy traveling, hiking, yoga, reading, photography, playing the saxophone, video games, board games, musicals, watching movies, and listening to music and podcasts.
